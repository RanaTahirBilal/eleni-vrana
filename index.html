She teaches finance because she has spent a career doing it.

Ten academic years as an adjunct lecturer at the Columbia University School of Professional Studies, taught alongside the corporate finance roles that supply the material.

Eleni Vrana is a Part-Time Lecturer of Finance at the Columbia University School of Professional Studies, where she teaches Introduction to Finance within the Enterprise Risk Management programme. She has lectured for the school for ten academic years.

The course is built on real-world examples and case studies rather than theory alone. That is possible because she was not teaching finance from a distance: for most of those ten years she was also working inside Columbia’s own finance function, and the problems she brought into the classroom were the ones she had been handed that week.

Before Columbia she spent more than a decade at Sotheby’s in New York, moving from credit analysis to client representation to account management between 1998 and 2008. It is an uncommon foundation for a finance career and a demanding one: at an auction house, valuation, terms and client relationships are inseparable, and a misjudgement is immediate and public.

Students in the Enterprise Risk Management programme are largely working professionals. They tend to be unforgiving about material that has never survived contact with a real balance sheet. That is the audience this course was built for.

About

Practice first. Then the classroom.

Eleni Vrana has more than twenty-five years of corporate finance experience. It began at Sotheby’s in New York, where valuation, credit and client terms sit inside the same conversation, and continued at Columbia University, where she spent seventeen years in its finance and procurement functions as Contract Officer and then Category Manager.

For ten of those years she was also teaching. That overlap is the reason her course works the way it does: the case studies are not historical, they are the work.

She holds an MBA from The George Washington University, a Master’s Business Certificate in Finance and Management from Columbia University, and a BS in Business Administration from the University of San Francisco. She was recognised by Marquis Who’s Who in 2026 and featured by Influential Women in July 2026.

A book, in progress.

Announced in her Influential Women feature, July 2026.

Eleni Vrana is co-authoring a forthcoming book on women’s success and happiness, built around the question of how to balance family, work and personal growth rather than choose between them.

It is the same subject her own career answers in practice: seventeen years inside a university’s finance function, ten of them taught in the evenings, without treating either as the thing that had to give.
