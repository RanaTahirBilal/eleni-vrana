II

Practice first. Then the classroom.

Eleni Vrana is a Part-Time Lecturer of Finance at the Columbia University School of Professional Studies, where she teaches Introduction to Finance within the Enterprise Risk Management programme. Her course runs on real world examples, projects and case study analyses rather than theory alone.

That is possible because she was not teaching finance from a distance. For most of those ten years she was also working inside Columbia’s own finance function, and the problems she brought into the room were the ones she had been handed that week.

Before Columbia she held several financial leadership positions at Sotheby’s New York, moving from credit analysis to client representation to account management. In her last role there she was an Account Manager, managing part of the financial aspects of all of the New York auctions.

It is an uncommon foundation for a finance career and a demanding one. At an auction house, valuation, credit and client terms sit inside the same conversation, and a misjudgement is immediate and public.
